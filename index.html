An AI assistant that stands in for you at meetings, with a live, photo-real avatar of you, a voice made from yours, and the judgement to know when to speak. It joins your real Zoom, Meet and Teams calls and speaks as you, and if someone sincerely asks, it says it's an AI.

How it works

Four steps from sign-up to stand-in.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Link Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, and tell zoope every name people call you.

  2. 02

    Teach

    Write the updates and facts it may share. Anything else becomes a follow-up, never a guess.

  3. 03

    Clone

    One face scan becomes a live avatar that lip-syncs to every word, and a 15-second recording becomes your voice.

  4. 04

    Confirm

    Approve each meeting. At the start time zoope opens its meeting room, and afterwards hands you a summary.

Judgement

Speaks when it's spoken to. Quiet when it isn't.

Every line in the meeting is scored. zoope answers when someone uses one of your names, asks the room something your notes cover, or follows up on what it just said. It holds back when a question is meant for someone else, or the speaker hasn't finished.

  • Recognises nicknames, even when speech-to-text misspells them
  • Turns requests into action items with due dates
  • Shows its reasoning for every decision

Features

Everything a stand-in needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Live avatar

Your real face from one scan, brought to life: it shapes each sound it says, blinks, glances and moves like you on camera.

Your voice

A neural model clones your voice from a 15-second sample and speaks any sentence in it.

Grounded answers

Answers only from the notes you write. Unknowns become follow-ups.

Action items

“Could you draft the email by Thursday?” lands in your summary with its due date.

Learns nicknames

Hears someone called by an unfamiliar name and asks whether it's you.

Confirm to join

Nothing joins on its own. You approve every meeting, one by one.

Privacy

Your face and voice never leave this device.

The decision engine, face model, avatar renderer and voice model all run in your browser. There are no API calls and no uploads. The voice model downloads once and is cached on your device.

    Join real meetings

    The zoope extension opens your meeting in the Zoom, Google Meet or Teams web app, joins with your name, and uses your avatar and voice as its camera and microphone.

    Not installedInstall it in Chrome or Edge to join actual calls.
    1. Download the extension and unzip it.
    2. In Chrome or Edge, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped zoope-extension folder.
    4. Reload this page. Add a meeting with its Zoom, Meet or Teams link, approve it, and press Join now.

    zoope follows the meeting through its live captions and turns them on when it can. It runs on this computer, so keep this tab open during the meeting.
