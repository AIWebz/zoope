Clone your face & voice

zoope maps 478 points on your face and animates your real photo, then builds your voice from you saying the alphabet. Nothing leaves this device.

1 Face clone

Turn on the camera, fit your face inside the oval, and press Scan face. You can also read the sentence to tune the backup voice.

2 Voice clone: say the alphabet

zoope shows one letter at a time. Say the letter's name clearly (“ay”, “bee”, “see”…), then pause. It moves on by itself. Every letter holds sounds zoope cuts out and reuses to speak in your voice. Click a letter to redo it.
